About Grill HQ

Grill HQ started with one charcoal grill and a simple idea: chicken should taste like it was grilled for you, because it was.

We marinate overnight, grill to order, and serve it with fries and sides that Lagosians actually love: yam, plantain, jollof, moi moi and proper pepper sauce.

Grill HQ was founded in 2022 by Maduka and Steven; two best friends who shared a passion for great food and bold flavours. What began as a small idea to serve delicious, affordable grilled meals soon grew into a vision of creating a go-to spot for satisfying meals made with quality ingredients and care.
